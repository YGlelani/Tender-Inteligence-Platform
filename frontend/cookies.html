Cookie Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective date: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what Tender Scrapper stores in your browser and why. It covers the Tender Scrapper website, dashboard and sign-up pages, and it should be read with our Privacy Policy.

1. Cookies and browser storage

A cookie is a small file that a website saves in your browser. Websites can also save information in your browser's local storage. Tender Scrapper uses one cookie and a few local storage items. All of them are first-party: only Tender Scrapper can read them, and we do not use them to follow you on other websites.

3. Categories

  • Necessary: needed to sign you in, keep your account secure and remember your cookie choice. The Service cannot work without them, so they cannot be switched off.
  • Functional: remember choices you make, such as light or dark mode. They are saved only when you make the choice, stay in your browser and are never sent to us.
  • Analytics: we do not use analytics or advertising cookies. If that changes, we will ask for your permission first.

4. Content from other companies

The website, dashboard and sign-up pages load nothing from other companies. The AI workspace page currently loads fonts from Google Fonts and a chart library from jsDelivr. When you open that page, your browser connects to those services, which receive your IP address and browser details under their own privacy policies.

5. Your choices

  • Change your cookie choice at any time with the Cookie preferences button at the bottom of the dashboard pages.
  • Log out to remove the sign-in items.
  • Delete everything Tender Scrapper has stored through your browser settings, for example by clearing the site's data. You will be signed out and your settings will reset.

6. Changes to this policy

If we change what we store, we will update this page and show the new version and effective date at the top.
