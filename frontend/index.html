Questions

Where do the tenders come from?

From the National Treasury eTender Portal (etenders.gov.za), where national and provincial departments, municipalities and state-owned entities publish their tenders.

How often is the list updated?

New tenders are collected regularly throughout the day. Closed tenders are hidden automatically, so what you see is still open.

How does matching work?

You tell us your industry, the provinces you work in, your keywords and your documents. Smart Alerts scores each tender against that profile and shows you the reasons for the score.

Can I choose which emails I get?

Yes. In your profile you can turn each type of notification on or off, in the app and by email.

Does Tender Scrapper submit bids for me?

No. It helps you find tenders, prepare and keep track. You submit your bid to the organisation as the tender notice describes.
